MEDICAL AI / 3D CT
01

RESEARCH PROTOTYPE · 2026

3D Colorectal Tumor Segmentation

A research codebase exploring binary CBAM-RSU2Net segmentation with a separate external colon anatomical prior for 3D colorectal CT volumes.

  • Binary tumor/background model direction based on label-value audit.
  • Sample-level prior geometry, visual review, and tumor-coverage analysis.
PyTorchMONAICBAM-RSU2NetNIfTI
View research repository ↗

The reported prior-coverage pilot uses three selected cases. It is not model Dice, clinical validation, or full-cohort performance. End-to-end model training and evaluation remain future work.

RESEARCH APPLICATION · 2026

Medical AI Research Workbench

A local-first application for reviewing 3D NIfTI CT volumes, inspecting segmentation overlays, checking spatial geometry, and calculating binary segmentation metrics.

  • Axial, coronal, and sagittal volume viewing.
  • Ground Truth / Colon Prior / AI Prediction overlays and metric calculations.
PythonStreamlitNiBabelPlotly
View application repository ↗

Current release is a viewer and evaluation workbench. It does not train or run CBAM-RSU2Net inference.
